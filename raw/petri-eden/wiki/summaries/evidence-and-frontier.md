## Active Frontier

The next instrumentation sequence is:

1. authoritative population and biomass sampling;
2. native charts, tables, and retained summaries;
3. material transfers, births, deaths, and cause evidence;
4. generation, trait, and controller-complexity aggregates;
5. integrity and distinct fault-state diagnostics;
6. long-run retention, storage/memory trends, and degraded-state verification.

This order matters. Later claims about species, evolution, or learning need a trustworthy measurement spine first.

## Current Human Decision

Predator viability is not yet satisfactory. Scripted predators die before the required long-run horizon because attacks carry health cost while usable energy arrives only through carcass scavenging. A human must choose the smallest ecology correction, likely one of:

- bounded direct energy transfer from successful predation;
- attack/metabolism/yield/decay rebalance;
- a minimal combination.

The change must preserve material conservation, plant/prey viability, deterministic worker-count digests, and the no-reward/no-fitness-score rule.

## Evidence Gates

Petri Eden should climb claims in order:

1. **execution** - builds and smoke runs;
2. **determinism** - repeated and worker-count digest agreement;
3. **viability** - long-run baselines and material conservation;
4. **measurement** - retained population, flow, trait, and integrity evidence;
5. **inheritance** - lineage/genome evidence across generations;
6. **repeatability** - fixed and unseen seeds, controls, ablations, failure cases;
7. **strong emergence claims** - operational definition and independent scrutiny.

The reusable guide is [[../../eval-trace/wiki/concepts/emergence-claim-ladder|Emergence Claim Ladder]].

## Baseline Design

Useful comparisons include:

- no-op organisms;
- random-action organisms;
- scripted foragers/predators;
- evolving controllers;
- fixed versus unseen seeds;
- `1/2/4` worker digest matrices;
- mechanism-on versus mechanism-off ablations;
- same ecology with different mutation/plasticity settings.

Random or no-op collapse can be a valid negative baseline. It becomes a problem only if a supposedly viable scripted policy also collapses or the result cannot be explained.

## Claims Not Yet Supported

- open-ended evolution;
- autonomous emergence of intelligence;
- stable species diversification in the biological sense;
- learned behavior in v1;
- emergent language or culture;
- production-ready unattended runs;
- installable release quality.
