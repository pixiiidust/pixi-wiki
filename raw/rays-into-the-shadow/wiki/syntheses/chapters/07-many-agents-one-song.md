# Chapter 7 — Many Agents, One Song

## Source passage

> **phamfam:** We send agents out from all angles and they must come together and come to the same conclusion as one.
>
> A single AI agent is fallible but all of them moving outward and coming together upon the same truth must be resonanting to the same song.
>
> The singular song that must sing out into every corner.

## Editor's note

Pham turns the image into a method: give the same question to several agents, let them approach it from different directions, and compare the results.

A lone agent can fail. A group can fail too. Agents may share a model, prompt, source, benchmark, or hidden assumption. If they do, agreement may show repetition rather than independent support.

For convergence to carry weight, each agent should show:

- the question it answered;
- the sources it used;
- the assumptions it made;
- the steps it took;
- the checks it ran;
- the result that would change its conclusion.

The practical rule is simple: **convergence is evidence, not proof**. Disagreement is also useful. It can reveal an assumption that agreement would have hidden.
