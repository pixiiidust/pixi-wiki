## Source passage

> **phamfam:** Began using AI 1 week before Fable 5 was released. Was late but wanted to understand and begin to see what was possible and began studying and obsessing over the behavior and nature.... no independent research on any of the AI literature... all fully independent and fully independent pathways and conclusions to the same unifying ideas.....

## Editor's note

The reference to “Fable 5” appears exactly as written. This edition does not guess what name or date Pham meant.

The passage makes three claims:

1. Pham had only recently begun to use AI.
2. He studied its behaviour closely.
3. He believes he reached ideas by his own route before reading the related AI literature.

The third point is a claim about his path, not independent proof of it. The attachments may help trace that path, but this edition has only their names and sizes.

This opening account becomes a conversation in Jamie's next turn. Jamie keeps the independent path in view and asks what stopped the work.
